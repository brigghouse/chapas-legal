Rules of your mates' round

Chapas · last updated: 3 November 2026

Almost all of Chapas is yours and nobody else's. Your mates' round is the exception: what you post there is seen by people. Few of them (only the mates you have accepted) and briefly (24 hours), but people all the same, so there are rules. The first time you post we ask you to accept them.

What you can post

The beer you're drinking: the cap, your rating, a comment, the place if you like and a photo of the beer. That is all the app lets you post, on purpose.

What you can't

  • Nothing offensive. Insults, harassment, hate towards anyone for any reason, threats. Not in the comment “as a joke” either.
  • No minors. Not in the photo, not behind the account. Chapas is for adults, full stop.
  • Nothing sexual or violent in photos.
  • No advertising, of beer or anything else. If you're a brewery and want to talk, write to us.
  • Nobody who doesn't want to be there. If someone else is in the photo, they should know and be fine with it.
  • Not a drop of pressure. Chapas rewards variety, not quantity: no drinking challenges, no bets on how many, no “who lasts longest”.

What happens to reports

  1. Anyone can report any story from inside the app, in two taps. On reporting, the story disappears from their screen at once, and they can block the author if they wish.
  2. We read reports ourselves (a person, not an algorithm) and normally act within 24 hours, which is how long a story lasts.
  3. If the story breaks these rules, we take it down for everyone.
  4. If someone does it again, or the first one is already serious (a minor, harassment), we close the account. You can appeal by writing to us.
  5. If the law requires us to pass something on, we do.

Blocking

Blocking someone cuts the link both ways: they don't see you and you don't see them, and they can't ask to be your mate again with that code. They aren't told. No explanation needed.

Always your call

  • Whether to post. The app never posts on its own or nudges you to.
  • Taking a story of yours down before it expires: one tap on it.
  • Who your mates are: only someone with your code, and only if you accept them.
  • Leaving. Deleting the account deletes your stories, your mates and your reports at once.
