Delete your account

Chapas · last updated: 25 October 2026

You can do it without asking anyone and without waiting for a reply. The quickest way is from the app itself, which deletes on the spot.

From the app · takes ten seconds

  1. Open Chapas and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete account, at the end of “Your data”.
  3. Confirm. Done: no queue, no review.

If you want to keep your album first, the same screen has Take my data with me: it gives you a file with everything.

If you no longer have the app installed

Write to us from your account's email address and we delete it for you. The button below opens your email app with the message already written:

Request deletion by email

It has to come from the account's address: that is the only way to know it is you asking and not someone deleting another person's album. If you signed in with Sign in with Apple and hid your email, write from that Apple account all the same: Apple's relay reaches us and that is enough.

What gets deleted

  • The account and the sign-in.
  • The whole album: beers, times noted, ratings, gifts and rounds.
  • Any cap photos that had been uploaded.

No copy and no “deactivated” version remains. The provider's backups rotate on their own and expire within 30 days at most.

What is not deleted from here

Whatever is stored only on your phone — if you used Chapas without an account, that is everything — goes when you delete the account from the app, or when you uninstall it. This page cannot reach your phone.

How long it takes

From the app, immediately. By email, as soon as we read it and always within the month the GDPR sets (art. 17); usually the same day.
